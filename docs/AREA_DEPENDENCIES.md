## First findings (2026-11-25)

*Hand-written; the script keeps this section when it regenerates the rest.*

**There are no layers yet, in either crate.** In core, 34 of 35 areas sit
in one cycle: from any of them you can follow links to any other and back.
Only `lib.rs` (the `Result` type) stands outside. The app is the same: 33
of 35 areas, everything except `deformers` (nothing uses it) and the test
helpers. It is not one bad link. Cutting every against-the-flow link that
only one file makes still leaves 31 core areas tangled; taking out the
`editor` and `scene_items` areas entirely still leaves 32. The tangle is
the sum of many small "reach up" links, so the fix is a steady ratchet,
not one big refactor.

**Why: two god structs.** Core methods are written as `impl Editor` blocks
in 83 files across most areas, and the `Editor` (`editor/`) names every
tool's types to dispatch, preview and undo them, so every tool area uses
`editor` and `editor` uses 26 other areas. The app does the same with the
`Operandi` struct in `main.rs`: every area adds `impl Operandi` blocks and
`main.rs` calls into every area. These two loops are the design for now,
which is why the CI check tracks *new* links rather than failing on all.

**Real layering mistakes in core, cheapest first:**

1. *Shared types filed in the wrong area* (done 2026-11-25: they and
   `CurveKind` now live in `geometry/polygon_kind.rs` and
   `geometry/component_modes.rs`). `PolygonRepresentation` lives
   in `primitives/cone.rs`, but `geometry` (`mesh.rs`,
   `topology_cache.rs`), `subdivision`, `transforms`, `tools` and
   `scene_items` all need it. The selection `Mode` enum lives in
   `scene_items/scene.rs` and is used by `geometry`, `subdivision`,
   `selections` (11 files) and `transforms` (13). `SetRef` sits in
   `selections`, but `geometry/mesh_attributes.rs` needs it. Moving these
   three types down into `geometry` removes the base area's worst upward
   links with no behaviour change.
2. *The base area calls tools* (done 2026-11-25: `polygon.rs`, with
   `cap_edit`, and `topology_cleanup.rs` moved to `polygons/`; `geometry`
   now reaches up only for the Solid Sketch data a mesh carries). `geometry` should be the floor, yet
   `geometry/polygon.rs` calls `bevel_geometry` (`polygons`), and
   `geometry/topology_cleanup.rs` uses `Editor`, `edge_commands` and
   `mesh_cleanup`: it is a tool filed in the wrong folder. Move it to
   `polygons/` and have the caller pass the bevel in. `mesh.rs` naming
   `curves` and `mesh_attributes.rs` naming `solid_sketch_store` are the
   other two.
3. *`scene.rs` owns every item kind.* `scene_items/scene.rs` alone reaches
   into materials, MaterialX, the mesh stack, painting, multires, render
   items, replicators, subdivision and selections. That makes `Scene` a
   hub: 53 areas (core and app) use `scene_items`, and it uses 20. Longer
   term, item kinds register themselves; short term, keep new item kinds
   from adding to it.
4. *Low-level areas reaching up to `editor` and `commands`:* `selections`
   (7 files use `Editor`), `transforms` (10), `tools` (9), `materials`
   (`shader_tree.rs`, `shading.rs`), `subdivision` (`FreeMemory`). Each is
   an `impl Editor` block that could live in the calling area instead.

**In the app, cheapest first:**

1. *`controls` reaching up.* The shared widgets should be the app's base,
   but `controls/option_row.rs` and `app_environment.rs` use
   `window_layout::form`, `tree_list.rs` uses `workspace`, three files use
   `tools::numeric_tab` and two use `settings::units_preferences`. Move
   those small pieces (form row types, unit formatting, numeric-tab
   helpers) down into `controls`.
2. *`window_layout` is the hub* (44 files, 38k lines; 29 areas use it, it
   uses 27). Its links back up into `settings` (14 files go the other
   way), `items`, `render` and `anim` are the ones to cut.
3. *`files` reaches into `settings`, `window_layout`, `controls` and
   `graphics`* (5, 5, 4 and 3 files): open/save code holding UI pieces.

**Feeding the to-do items:**

- *Split the 3D-view giants.* `view` (34 files) and `viewport` (24) are
  not layered: `viewport` uses `view` in 9 files and `view` reaches back
  into `viewport` in 2 (`view/handle_tips.rs`, `view/slice_view.rs`). Cut

  `path_tool_view.rs` and `duplicate_view.rs` also reach into the

  that belongs with its tool.
- *Turn folders into real modules.* Start with the areas few others name,
  since only their callers' paths change: in core `meshblend_spike` (one
  file uses it), `openpbr`, `solid_sketch`, `lxo`, `bake`, `meshblend`,
  `materialx` (4-6 areas each); in the app `deformers` (none), `nodes`,
  `transforms`, `duplication` (one each). `scene_items`, `commands`,
  `editor`, `geometry` and `window_layout` go last.
- *Shrink the shading files.* `core/materials` is 19 files and 18.9k
  lines; `shader_eval.rs` (4.6k) and `shader_tree.rs` (3.9k) carry most of
  it, plus `app/materials/shading_panel.rs` (4.4k). `shader_tree.rs`
  reaches into 8 areas (`Scene`, `Editor`, `user_defaults`, procedurals,
  node graphs, render outputs). Split it along those lines: the tree data,

  panel uses 10 app areas from one file; its render, preset and file
  sections are the natural cut lines.
- *Tidy-ups.* `meshblend_spike` is used by one file
  (`meshblend/strips.rs`) yet reaches into 8 areas: move the one piece
  `strips.rs` needs into `meshblend` and the spike can go (or move to
  tests). Painting, though "parked", is wired into the core: `editor.rs`,
  `editor/command.rs`, `editor/history.rs`, `scene_items/scene.rs` and
  `sculpting/sculpt.rs` name it, and so do 5 app areas outside
  `painting_ui`. Parking it properly means those links go through one
  seam. The icon studio is its own crate, outside this check.

**What to fix first:** the three misplaced core types (core item 1), then
`geometry/topology_cleanup.rs` and the `bevel_geometry` call (core item
2), then `controls` (app item 1) and the two `view → viewport` links. Each
fix deletes lines from `scripts/area-deps-baseline.txt`. Once the core
base (`geometry`, `subdivision`, `selections`) is out of the cycle, switch
the CI step to `--strict`.
