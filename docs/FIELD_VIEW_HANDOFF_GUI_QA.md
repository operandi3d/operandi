# Fields and views take turns: GUI checklist

For Jason or Codex (Claude can't click Operandi). Use the QA app named in
the thread. Repeat steps 1-5 with Cube, Sphere and Cylinder.

1. Create tab, pick the tool and draw the shape in the Perspective view.
2. Click Size X (Radius X), type 2, press Tab. The next field opens.
   Move the pointer into the view and press A: the view frames the shape
   and the field closes (no "a" typed into it).
3. Drag the shape's X arrow: it moves.
4. Click Position Y, type 1, press Return. Drag a side handle: it works.
5. Click Position X, type 3 and, without Return, press on the X arrow and
   drag: the shape jumps to X 3 and then follows the drag.
6. Click a field, type 1, slide the pointer over the view and press A:
   the field keeps the keyboard, so "a" goes into it and the view doesn't
   frame. Backspace, then Return confirms 1.
7. Cone (Create tab): type Position X 25, Return, pointer over the view,
   A: the cone preview fills the view.
8. Move tool on a selection: type into a field, Tab, pointer over the
   view, A frames.
9. Cylinder and Sphere: press anywhere along a move arrow (not only its
   tip) and drag: the shape moves along that axis. The squares still
   resize it.
10. In a scene with other objects, pick Cube/Sphere/Cylinder and press A
    before drawing: the view closes in on the tool's centre (about a 1 m
    box). Draw the shape and press A: it frames the new shape only.
