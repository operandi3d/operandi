# Code of conduct

Operandi is made by people who love modelling and want to share that. Be
the kind of collaborator you'd want to work with.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to 3D, Rust or
  Git. Everyone was new once.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give credit, take feedback, and admit mistakes, including clean-room
  slips (see [docs/CLEAN_ROOM.md](docs/CLEAN_ROOM.md)).
- Respect people's time: say what you need clearly and follow through on
  what you take on.

## What isn't OK

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning remarks about anyone's
  identity, background, body or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Pressuring anyone to break the Modo ground rules or the licences.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring a maintainer's request to
  stop.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions) and
anywhere you represent Operandi, such as project chats and events.

## Reporting and what happens next

Tell Jason (GitHub: [manfriday](https://github.com/manfriday)) privately,
using the contact on his GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.
Jason may edit or remove comments, warn, or ban someone from the project,
depending on what happened. If a report is about Jason, say so and it will
be handled by another long-standing contributor.

This code is inspired by the Contributor Covenant.
